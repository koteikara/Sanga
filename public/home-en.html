Getting to the stadium

AFC Champions League: In AFC matches the venue is listed as Kyoto Stadium (京都府立京都スタジアム, its official name). It is the same stadium as Sanga Stadium by KYOCERA.

In short

  1. Kyoto Station
  2. JR Sagano Line to Kameoka Station, about 20 min
  3. About 3 min on foot from Kameoka Station

There is no stadium parking for fans on match days. Please come by public transport.

Official: Sanga Stadium access (Japanese)

Coming by car

On J.League match days and event days, the stadium car park is for organisers and staff only. There is no parking for fans. Please come by public transport. If you still come by car, the options are below.
